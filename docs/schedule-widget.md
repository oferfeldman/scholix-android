# Schedule widget

Add **Scholix → Schedule** from the launcher's widget picker, then choose a saved schedule provider. The daily view uses the app's subject colors, icons, lesson details, and free-period rules. After the last active lesson ends, it shows tomorrow's schedule. Cancelled lessons and subjects marked as free periods do not delay that switch.

Tap the refresh arrow to fetch changes, the gear to choose another provider, or the heading or a lesson to open the schedule in Scholix. The lesson list scrolls and the widget can be resized.

Background refresh runs every 15 minutes, with additional updates when the schedule screen opens, at the end of the school day, at midnight, and after clock or timezone changes. Android can defer background work to save battery; this is not a real-time push feed. A failed refresh retains cached lessons and shows a warning and the last successful update time. Reconnect the account in Scholix if its session has expired.
